About us

Born for hotels.

Hospitality Minds was formed to simplify the life of hoteliers. This portfolio brings together the creative side of that work — the films, photographs, campaigns and websites behind the brands we grow.

Who we are

One partner, every touchpoint.

Hospitality Minds is a Mumbai-based hotel marketing company working with hotels, resorts, restaurants and serviced apartments across India and beyond. Alongside revenue management, OTA and digital marketing, our creative team plans and produces the content that represents each property online.

Because the same team understands bookings, rankings and guest behaviour, every shoot and every page is built with a purpose: to help the right guests choose your property.

Creative philosophy

What guides the work.

01

Guest-first storytelling

We frame every property the way a guest discovers it — arrival, room, view, plate, memory — so the content answers the questions people ask before they book.

02

True to the stay

Real spaces, real light, real food. Content that matches the experience builds trust, reduces surprises and earns better reviews.

03

Made for where it lives

OTA galleries, Instagram, Google and your own website each need something different. We plan every frame, cut and page for its channel.

Our process

From first call to final cut.

A clear, collaborative workflow — so you always know what is happening next.

  1. 01

    Discover

    A site visit or call to understand the property, its guests, its competition and what makes it different.

  2. 02

    Strategise

    Shot lists, content calendars and sitemaps built around the rooms, experiences and offers that drive bookings.

  3. 03

    Create

    Photography, films, reels and creatives produced on location by our in-house team and creator partners.

  4. 04

    Develop

    Websites and landing pages designed and built mobile-first, with booking engines and enquiry flows connected.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Content published across your website, OTAs, Google and social channels — consistently and on brand.

  6. 06

    Optimise

    Performance reviewed regularly so the next shoot, campaign or page builds on what is working.
