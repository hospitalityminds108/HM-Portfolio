Overview

The story.

Hotel Parle International needed imagery that could carry every channel at once: OTA listings, the hotel website, Google and social. We photographed each room category alongside the public spaces that define the stay — the carved-timber restaurant, the lounge and the reception.

To support banquet and wedding enquiries, we added a short vertical wedding promo built with AI-generated, miniature-style imagery — an eye-catching format designed for Instagram and WhatsApp sharing.
