Overview

The story.

For Manas Lifestyle Resort we produced a landscape overview film that moves from arrival through rooms, dining and facilities, built for the resort website and OTA galleries.

Every villa and room category — from Luxe Rooms and suites to the multi-bedroom Ubud, Urban and Royal villas — has its own single-take vertical walkthrough, so guests can see the exact layout before they book.

In July 2025 our crew was on location at the resort; the behind-the-scenes film documents the planning, styling and camera work that goes into a Hospitality Minds production day.
