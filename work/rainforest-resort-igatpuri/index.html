Overview

The story.

Rainforest Resort sits in the hills of Igatpuri, a weekend escape from Mumbai and Nashik, with lawns and banquet spaces set against the mountains. The content we produced covers the full funnel — an overview film for the website, a wedding venue film and décor reels for social, and a destination-wedding creative for paid and organic campaigns.
